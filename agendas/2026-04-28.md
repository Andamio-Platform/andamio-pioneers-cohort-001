## Andamio Pioneer Program Meeting Agenda

> Cohort #001, Meeting #10 (2026-04-28) — **Final Session (Week 10 of 10)**

---

## 1. Check-In Round

Short — 1–2 minutes each.

**Closing prompt:** *What's one thing you can do now that you couldn't do ten weeks ago?*

## 2. "Tell a Friend" Round (15 min)

Last week's homework: *what would I tell a friend about Andamio after this cohort?*

Each pioneer shares their answer. We capture the language verbatim — these become the candidate sentences we test in onboarding, on the site, and in proposals. Your words, not ours.

## 3. Topic A — Incentivizing Open-Source Contribution (20 min)

Teed up at Meeting #08. Timely: Andamio is preparing a proposal to fund open-source onboarding paths and ecosystem support. The question this cohort is uniquely positioned to answer:

- Where does Andamio's "earned then given" credential model fit OSS contribution incentives?
- What's broken about the current model (Catalyst, bounty platforms, GitHub sponsors)?
- Would *you* contribute to an OSS project differently if your contribution was recorded as a portable credential?
- What's the line between "rewarded contribution" and "gamified noise"?

Output: a few sharp sentences we can carry into the proposal narrative.

## 4. Topic B — When Does "Andamio" Get in the Way? (15 min)

Also from Meeting #08. The naming question:

- When does saying "Andamio" make a conversation harder rather than easier?
- Are we selling a platform, a credential, a contribution primitive, or something else?
- What language do *you* reach for when you describe what we do?

This connects to Topic A — the proposal needs to lead with the problem, not the brand.

## 5. Cohort Retrospective (15 min)

What worked, what didn't, what's missing.

Three rounds, one minute each:
1. **Worked:** what should the next cohort definitely keep?
2. **Didn't:** what should we drop or rebuild?
3. **Missing:** what would have made the program 2x more valuable?

## 6. What's Next (10 min)

- **Alumni status:** what does it mean to have completed Cohort #001? Credential, ongoing role, both?
- **Cohort #002:** timing, shape, who recruits — your input matters here
- **Stay involved:** issues repo, andamio-dev contributor pipeline, ecosystem onboarding paths in flight
- **The Intersect proposal:** if it lands, this cohort's feedback shaped it — public credit, ongoing involvement opportunities

## 7. Acknowledgments + Close (5 min)

You are the first cohort of Andamio Pionners. Thank you!

- One concrete thing you want to keep working on — alone, with us, or with another pioneer
